What does your scooter cost you per kilometre?

From your maintenance costs and odometer readings, ScootKeeper works out the cost per kilometre – and compares it with what the same distance would have cost by public transport or by car. You can try the same calculation right here.

The comparison figures are rough reference values for orientation, not an up-to-date calculation: public transport 12 ct/km (German Deutschlandticket at an assumed 500 km per month), car 35 ct/km (ADAC cost estimate for a small car including depreciation, insurance, fuel and maintenance).

How healthy is your battery?

From a single charge – the energy charged and the state of charge before and after – ScootKeeper extrapolates the actual capacity and compares it with the rated capacity of your battery. You can try this calculation here as well.

– battery health
–estimated actual capacity
–compared to the rated capacity

Charge ranges below 10 percentage points are too imprecise to extrapolate from, so the app deliberately returns no result for them. If no capacity data is available at all, the app estimates from the cycle count instead (about 4 % loss per 100 full cycles, floored at 50 %). Both are estimates, not a laboratory measurement.

Privacy

ScootKeeper works without an account and without our own server. Your entries are stored locally on your device. There are no ads, no tracking and no sharing of your data.

To keep the imprint and the privacy policy up to date, the app fetches those texts from GitHub at most once a day. For technical reasons your IP address is transmitted to GitHub in the process; no app content is sent. Without an internet connection the bundled versions are shown.

The free local ZIP backup can be saved or shared through an app of your choice. With Pro you can additionally back up your data to your own Google Drive account – transferred over an encrypted connection (TLS) and stored in an app-private area invisible to other apps. Only you have access; we never receive your data.

Pricing and transparency

ScootKeeper Pro is a subscription and is currently marked as “Coming soon”. It adds convenient Google Drive backup to the free local backup. Every other feature shown here is free to use.

No brokering, no commission. ScootKeeper does not broker any products or services and contains no affiliate or partner links. No commission is paid, and there are no advertising partners. The app is funded solely through the optional Pro subscription.

Please note

ScootKeeper is a self-organisation tool and does not replace professional servicing by a workshop. The cost comparisons on this page are for rough orientation and are not a binding calculation.
